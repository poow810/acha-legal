Delete your account

App: A-CHA (com.acha.app) · Last updated: 2026-07-25

You can delete your A-CHA account and its data at any time. There are two ways: from inside the app, or by emailing us if you no longer have the app installed.

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open A-CHA and sign in.
  2. Go to Me → Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and you are signed out. It cannot be undone.

Option 2 — By email

Send a request to syg7974@gmail.com from the email address registered on your account, with the subject “Account deletion request”.

We verify that the request comes from the account owner, then delete the account. We complete requests within 30 days, and normally much sooner. We will reply to confirm when it is done.

If you are unsure whether a deletion request went through, or you want a copy of your data before deleting, email syg7974@gmail.com.
