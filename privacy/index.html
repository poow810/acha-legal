Introduction

아차코리아 (ACHA KOREA) ("A-CHA", "we") runs the A-CHA mobile app and the website a-cha.app (the "Service"). This policy explains how we handle personal data under the Korean Personal Information Protection Act and how to reach us. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not track you for advertising.

Article 1 (Why we process personal data)

We process personal data only for the purposes below. If a purpose changes, we tell you first and ask for consent where required.

  • Accounts: confirming who you are, sign-in, managing your account, preventing abuse, and closing accounts
  • Community: showing your profile (only the fields you choose), boards, chats, friends and flash meetups
  • A-CHA events: booking, payment and payment checks, tickets and entry, cancellations and refunds
  • Partner services: passing club event and dues sign-ups, and booking requests, to that club or vendor
  • Split bills: keeping expense records and showing the payout details you enter to the people you split with
  • Answering your questions and sending the notifications you turn on
  • Safety: handling reports and blocks, school verification, and responding to illegal activity
  • Legal duties, such as keeping transaction records

Article 6 (Who can see your information)

  • Other users see only your nickname and the profile fields you have enabled. Nationality, stay and school can each be turned on or off. School needs verification and is off by default. Friend cards are hidden until you turn them on.
  • Profile links: by default only you and your accepted friends can see the usernames and numbers you add. Other users see which apps you have linked (their logos) and nothing else, and the username is not sent to their device, unless you share that link on the web (below). People you have blocked, and people who have blocked you, see nothing in the app.
  • Links on the web (optional): if you turn on "Share my links on the web" in Edit profile, anyone, including people who are not signed in, can see the links you choose on your page at a-cha.app/u/… and in the app. Your WhatsApp number is never shown there. That page cannot tell who is looking, so blocking someone does not hide your links on it. Turn sharing off and the links are removed right away.
  • Live location is visible only to the people in the chat you started it in, and only while it is running.

Article 7 (Location)

We use your device location only when you open a nearby map or share a location in a chat, and never in the background. On a nearby map, your location is used on your device to center the map. A pin you choose to share is stored as your chat message. Live location is always opt-in: you pick how long it runs (15 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours, extendable up to 8 hours in total), it updates only in that chat, you can stop it any time from the banner in the chat, and it stops by itself when the time runs out.

Article 8 (How we delete data)

When the retention period ends or the purpose is fulfilled, we delete the data without delay. Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered, and we keep no paper records. Data kept by law is stored separately and deleted when its period ends. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your profile, posts, comments, reviews, chat messages and verification documents.

Article 9 (Your rights)

  • You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your personal data, to stop its processing, or to withdraw your consent.
  • In the app, change what your profile shows and your notifications in Settings, and delete your account in Settings.
  • Add, change or remove your profile links, or turn web sharing on or off, any time in Edit profile.
  • For anything else, contact acha0516@naver.com or Contact us (a-cha.app/support) and we will act without delay. A legal representative or someone you authorize can also ask for you. If you cannot use the app, follow the email instructions on our account deletion page.

Article 10 (Children under 14)

Children under 14 cannot join without the consent of a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 14 without that consent, and we delete it without delay if we find out.

Article 11 (How we keep data safe)

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and database access control so that members can read and write only their own data (row-level security)
  • Keeping admin access to a minimum and logging admin actions
  • Partly masking emails, names, accounts and contact details in staff alerts
  • Deleting school verification documents right after review

Article 12 (Cookies and browser storage)

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The website keeps your language choice, test results and sign-in state in your browser's storage, which you can clear in your browser settings. Clearing it signs you out and removes saved test results.
