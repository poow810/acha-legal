Child Safety Standards

App: A-CHA (com.acha.app) · Last updated: 2026-08-01

A-CHA is a community app for international exchange students living in Korea. These are our published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Zero tolerance. A-CHA prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation of any kind. We remove violating content, permanently terminate the accounts responsible, preserve evidence, and report to the relevant authorities.

1. Who A-CHA is for

A-CHA is intended for adults aged 18 and over, and its Google Play target audience is set to 18+. Accounts we identify as belonging to minors are removed.

2. What is prohibited

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), in any form, including drawn, animated, or computer-generated depictions.
  • Sexualization of minors, including sexualized commentary about a minor.
  • Grooming, solicitation, sextortion, or any attempt to make sexual contact with a minor.
  • Trafficking of minors, or facilitating or advertising such activity.
  • Sharing, requesting, or linking to any of the above, on or off the app.

These rules are also stated in our Terms of Service, which every user must accept before creating an account.

3. How to report

In the app — every post, comment, chat message, profile, and product review has a Report option. Choose “Child safety concern” to route the report to our priority queue. You can also block any user at any time.

4. How we respond

  1. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team, typically within 24 hours. Child safety reports are handled first.
  2. Violating content is removed and the account is permanently terminated.
  3. Relevant data is preserved as evidence for law enforcement.
  4. We report to the competent authorities, including the Korean National Police Agency and, where applicable, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  5. Content that receives repeated reports is automatically hidden pending review.

5. Preventive measures

  • Semi-real-name community. Users are identified by a nickname, nationality, and — if verified — a school. Anonymous accounts are not the default.
  • Report and block are available on all user-generated content and on every profile.
  • 18+ audience and no features designed for or directed at children.
  • Server-side enforcement. Blocking and content removal are enforced in the database, not only in the app.
  • Accounts terminated for child safety violations are not permitted to return.

6. Legal compliance

A-CHA complies with applicable child safety laws in the jurisdictions where it operates, including the Republic of Korea’s Act on the Protection of Children and Youth against Sex Offences (아동·청소년의 성보호에 관한 법률) and the Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection (정보통신망법). Where a report concerns a user outside Korea, we cooperate with the competent authority in that jurisdiction.

7. Emergencies

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. In Korea, dial 112 (police) or 119 (emergency). Then notify us so we can preserve evidence.
